The copy voice belongs only to this business. It is the Ohio county mineral docket: a calm, exact business voice built around the deed chain, reservation language, recorded notice, Dormant Mineral Act questions, paid decimals, Utica and Point Pleasant well evidence, written conveyance terms, and a recorder-ready closing file.
